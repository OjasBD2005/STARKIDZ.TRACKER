🤖 How auto-tasking works

  • Sample sent → "Check-in on sample feedback" in 7 days + moves to Sample stage.
  • Follow up call → task in 3 days.
  • Price negotiation → "Send revised quotation" in 2 days → Negotiation.
  • Order received → "Confirm & enter in Sales Tracker" tomorrow → Won.
  • Keywords in your notes (sample, rate, order…) also trigger tasks.

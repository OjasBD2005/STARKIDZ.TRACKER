🔗 Inventify — Raw Material Inventory Sync

Connect to Inventify to automatically pull raw material stock levels (upper, sole, components). Inventory data syncs into the Stock List and updates article-wise raw material indicators.

How it works:
  • Enter your Inventify API key and click Sync Now
  • Raw material stock (upper, sole, PVC granules, adhesive, etc.) is pulled automatically
  • Each article shows a raw material availability icon on the Orders screen
  • Low/out-of-stock materials trigger alerts on the Dashboard
  • Stock movement log records every Inventify sync with timestamp

📦 Busy — Item Master Sync

Connect to Busy accounting software to sync the item master list (finished products, article codes, SKUs).

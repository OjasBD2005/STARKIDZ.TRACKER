📤 Upload / Configure Finished Goods Stock

Upload an .xlsx / .csv (not PDF). Works with: your Busy Stock report (Item Name + STOCK columns — grouped/size-level rows are auto-summed per article, even when headers aren’t on row 1), a raw SKU export (Item SkuCode + Qty), or a summary file (Article, Stock (ctns), Stock (pairs)). Tip: in Busy, export the stock report as Excel rather than printing to PDF. If a file doesn’t match, you’ll now get a clear message instead of a silent no-op.

🗂️ Stock Catalogue — Article / Colour / Size

Live stock laid out like the sample catalogue — article photo, colour-wise rows and size-wise quantities with MRP. Salespersons can browse, search and print/save as PDF while taking orders.

⬆️ Upload daily stock sheet

Export the stock report from Busy as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV — the same report used for the catalogue, with Item Name, Colour, Size, MRP and the quantity column. Grouped (blank) cells are filled down and Total rows are skipped automatically. The catalogue, aging and photo matching all refresh the moment it uploads.

🔗 Inventify — Raw Material Inventory Sync

Connect to Inventify to automatically pull raw material stock levels (upper, sole, components). Inventory data syncs into the Stock List and updates article-wise raw material indicators.

How it works:
  • Enter your Inventify API key and click Sync Now
  • Raw material stock (upper, sole, PVC granules, adhesive, etc.) is pulled automatically
  • Each article shows a raw material availability icon on the Orders screen
  • Low/out-of-stock materials trigger alerts on the Dashboard
  • Stock movement log records every Inventify sync with timestamp

📦 Busy — Item Master Sync

Connect to Busy accounting software to sync the item master list (finished products, article codes, SKUs).

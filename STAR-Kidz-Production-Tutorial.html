1 & 2 — System Overview & Login

What this system does and how to access it

What is the STAR Kidz Production System?

The STAR Kidz Production Planning & Tracking System is the internal portal used to manage the complete manufacturing lifecycle — from the moment a Sales Order arrives from the Sales team, through all production stages, to final dispatch.

Two apps work together:
• Sales App (ojas-dispatch-tracker) — Sales team enters orders, tracks status, allows dispatch.
• Production App (this system) — Production team receives orders, plans, executes, and reports completion back to Sales in real time.

Both apps automatically sync every 4–5 seconds via a shared link (Firebase/localStorage). No manual export or WhatsApp needed.

How to Login
  1. 1Open login.html in your browser (or the shared link/PWA icon on your phone).
  2. 2Select your name from the dropdown list.
  3. 3Enter your password. Each role has a unique password (see table below).
  4. 4Click Login. You will be redirected to the Production System automatically.
  5. 5The system loads your role-specific view — you only see what is relevant to your job.

Section 6 — Pending Orders: Allotting a New Sales Order

When the Sales team places a new order, it appears in 📥 Pending Orders waiting for you to review and allot it to a production department.

  1. 1Click 📥 Pending Orders in the sidebar. A red badge shows how many orders need attention.
  2. 2Review each order card: Order No, Customer, Article, Size Range, Qty, Delivery Date, Priority.
  3. 3Click Allot Department. A form appears — select the Moulding Category:
    PU PVC Stuckon Eva Crocs Injection
  4. 4Enter the Committed Completion Date — the date you promise to complete this order by.
  5. 5Optionally tick "Mark as Planned" if you have already planned the machine/shift for this order.
  6. 6Click Save Allotment. The order moves to Production Orders and the Sales team immediately sees 🎯 Komal committed [date] on their side.
  7. 7The correct department supervisor is automatically assigned (e.g., PVC → Supervisor 3) and the order appears in their queue.
If a new order arrives but the article is not yet in the system, contact Sushil (Planning) to add the article and BOM first.
Section 7 — GM Approval Workflow

When a supervisor completes packing on an order and requests GM sign-off, a notification appears on your screen. The order will also show a blue badge on ✅ GM Approval in the sidebar.

  1. 1Click ✅ GM Approval in the sidebar. You will see all orders that have requested completion.
  2. 2Each order shows: Order details, department, quantity made, packing %, completion date.
  3. 3Review the order. If satisfied, click ✅ Approve & Send to Dispatch.
    If there is a problem, click Send Back with a remark explaining what needs to be fixed.
  4. 4Once approved, the Sales team sees the order status change to In Stock — Awaiting Dispatch Allowance.
  5. 5The Sales person then clicks Allow Dispatch and the order moves to Supervisor 8 (Dispatch).
Key Rule: An order CANNOT be dispatched unless:
(1) Production marked it 100% packed → (2) Komal approves → (3) Sales allows dispatch → (4) Supervisor 8 dispatches.

10–13 — For Sushil (Planning & Billing)

Machine Plans, Upper Plans, Article Plans, Packing Plans

Section 10 — Machine Running Plan

The Machine Running Plan is the daily production schedule for each machine. Click ⚙️ Machine Running Plan under Production Planning.

  1. 1Click ➕ New Plan to create a machine run plan.
  2. 2Select the Date (defaults to today).
  3. 3Select the Machine. The system auto-fills the Category based on the machine's department.
    PVC — ROTARY machines: COSMO, SPORTS, EAGLE, MAX
    PVC — VERTICLE machines: ASH, TOHHLER, COCO … VICTORIA + STAR series
    EVA — STANLEY: STANLEY-1, STANLEY-2, STANLEY-201, STANLEY-601
  4. 4Select the Article (auto-populated from the mould map for that machine).
  5. 5Enter Target Qty (pairs) for this run.
  6. 6Select Shift (Day / Night).
  7. 7Click Add Plan. A Job Card is generated — print it and give it to the relevant supervisor.
Filter options: Use the dropdowns at the top to filter plans by Period (Daily / Weekly / Monthly), Category (PU / PVC / Stuckon / Eva Crocs Injection), and Date. Each supervisor only sees their own department's plans.
StatusMeaning

Section 11 — Upper Planning

Click 🧵 Upper Planning to plan and track upper-making (cutting upper fabric and stitching).

  1. 1Click ➕ New Upper Plan.
  2. 2Select Date, Category, Article.
  3. 3Select Upper Mode: In-house (our workers) or Out-house (contractor).
    If Out-house, also enter the Vendor / Contractor name.
  4. 4Enter Qty planned and Target date.
  5. 5Save. This plan appears in Supervisor 1's view for execution.
Section 12 — Article Planning

Click 📋 Article Planning to plan production targets article-by-article for a given period.

  1. 1Filter by Date, Category, Article as needed.
  2. 2Click ➕ New Article Plan: select date, category, article, planned qty.
  3. 3The plan table shows article name, planned qty, actual produced, variance, and status.
  4. 4If actual production is less than planned, a Variance is flagged — the supervisor must log a reason.
Section 13 — Packing Planning

Click 📦 Packing Planning to plan and record packing targets.

  1. 1Click ➕ Add Packing Plan: enter date, category, article, ready qty (pairs).
  2. 2The plan links to the corresponding order and updates the packing % on the Production Orders view.
  3. 3When packing reaches 100%, the supervisor can request GM Approval from the order card.
Packing progress directly drives the Sales team's view — they can watch it go from 0% → 100% in real time.

14–18 — Department Supervisors: Upper, PU, PVC, Stuckon, Eva

Step-by-step guide for production supervisors

Section 14 — Supervisor 1: Upper Department

Role: Managing upper-making (fabric cutting + stitching) and issuing uppers to the moulding department.

  1. 1Login as SUPERVISOR 1. You can see all orders (not just one dept).
  2. 2Go to 📜 Production Orders. Find orders where Cutting is done (cutting stage shows ✅).
  3. 3Click Update Upper on an order. Enter pairs completed in the Upper stage and save.
  4. 4Go to 🧵 Upper Issuance. Assign the upper-making job:
    • In-House — your own workers
    • Out-House — give to a contractor (enter contractor name, qty, target date)
  5. 5Once upper-making is complete, mark the Upper stage as 100%. The order moves to Moulding stage.
  6. 6Check 📥 My Pending Queue for material allotments from RM Store — click Acknowledge when you receive the material.
Section 15 — Supervisor 2: PU Department

Role: Moulding and packing of PU (Polyurethane) shoe orders on PU injection machines.

  1. 1Login as SUPERVISOR 2. Only PU orders are visible.
  2. 2Open Production Orders. Find your orders (shows only PU allotted orders).
  3. 3Click Update Moulding: enter pairs produced today. The % updates automatically.
  4. 4If production is less than planned, a Variance form appears — enter the reason (machine breakdown, material issue, mould defect, etc.).
  5. 5Once moulding is done, click Update Packing and enter pairs packed into cartons.
  6. 6When packing = 100%, click Request GM Approval. Komal will receive the notification.
  7. 7Check 📥 My Pending Queue and acknowledge RM Store allotments for your orders.
PU machines: CRETA, PUB
Section 16 — Supervisor 3: PVC Department

Role: Moulding and packing of PVC compound orders — both chappals (ROTARY) and shoes (VERTICLE).

PVC uses TWO machine types — both are PVC compound:
• ROTARY machines (4 machines): COSMO, SPORTS, EAGLE, MAX — for chappals/slippers
• VERTICLE machines (23 machines): ASH, TOHHLER, COCO, FIRE BLOT, JSS, LUKE, SONIC, RAFALE, AVIATION, RANGER, JAZZY, GCS, BEAR, BEAST, DASH, ALLA, CANDY, CRYSTA, JESSICA, KAREENA, KIARA, KITTO, VICTORIA + STAR series — for shoes
  1. 1Login as SUPERVISOR 3. You see only PVC orders.
  2. 2Check Machine Running Plan for your shift: ⚙️ Machine Running Plan → filter by PVC.
  3. 3Update Moulding progress on each active order (enter pairs produced per machine run).
  4. 4Enter Moulding last given date — the date you last handed off to packing.
  5. 5Update Packing progress. When 100% packed, request GM Approval.
Section 17 — Supervisor 4: Stuckon Department

Role: Moulding and packing of Stuckon orders (glued/bonded sole construction).

  1. 1Login as SUPERVISOR 4. You see only Stuckon orders.
  2. 2Stuckon machines: ITALY, NEO, ROME, MARS, SIGMA, SPEED, TYSON, KSJ, DIYA, GSJ, OJASVI, SKID, EVA-GOLA.
  3. 3Update Moulding and Packing progress the same way as PU/PVC supervisors (steps above).
  4. 4Acknowledge material from RM Store via 📥 My Pending Queue.
  5. 5When 100% packed, click Request GM Approval.

  3. 3For each order, update Moulding progress: enter pairs injected today per machine.
  4. 4Update Packing progress as pairs are packed by size set.

Section 19 — Supervisor 6: Cutting Incharge

Role: The first stage in the production pipeline. Cutting fabric/materials before upper-making begins.

  1. 1Login as SUPERVISOR 6. Go to 📜 Production Orders.
  2. 2For each new order, click Update Cutting.
  3. 3Set Cutting Mode: In-house (factory cutters) or Contractor (outsourced cutting).
  4. 4If Contractor: enter Contractor Name and Fabricator Type (Job Worker / Complete Upper Maker).
  5. 5Enter Last Delivery Date to Upper — the date you sent/will send cut material to Supervisor 1 (Upper).
  6. 6Save. The order moves to Upper Production stage. Supervisor 1 is notified.
You can also access 👥 People & Contractors to view and manage the contractor directory.

20–21 — Raw Material Store & Dispatch

Supervisor 7 and Supervisor 8 guide

Section 20 — Supervisor 7: Raw Material Store

Role: Managing raw material inventory, checking BOM requirements, and allotting materials to departments.

A. BOM Stock Check

  1. 1Click 📋 BOM Stock Check under Raw Material Store in the sidebar.
  2. 2For each active order, the system shows: Material required vs available stock vs shortfall.
  3. 3If stock is sufficient: click ⚡ Quick Allot to allot material to the relevant department.
  4. 4If stock is insufficient: log a Pendency — enter how much is short, expected arrival date, and a narration explaining the delay.

B. Allotment Queue

  1. 1Click 📤 Allotment Queue to see all material allotments you have issued.
  2. 2Each entry shows: material name, qty allotted, destination department, and whether the supervisor has Acknowledged receipt.
  3. 3Once a supervisor acknowledges receipt, the row turns green (Received).

C. Inventory Management

  1. 1Click 📊 Inventory to see all stock items with current level, minimum level, and category.
  2. 2Click + Add Stock on any item to record new material received. This increases the stock count.
  3. 3Click 📝 Movement Log to see a complete history of all stock additions and deductions with reasons.
Items shown in red are out of stock. Items in amber are at or below minimum level. Alert Sushil (Planning) immediately for urgent reorder.
Section 21 — Supervisor 8: Dispatch

Role: Physically dispatching completed orders that have been approved by GM and allowed by Sales.

  1. 1Login as DISPATCH 1. Go to 📜 Production Orders.
  2. 2You will only see orders with status Sent to Dispatch (GM approved + Sales allowed).
  3. 3Click Dispatch on an order. Fill in:
    • Vehicle No. (truck/tempo number)
    • Pairs dispatched (can be partial — multiple dispatch batches are supported)
    • Dispatch Date
  4. 4Click Confirm Dispatch. The Sales team sees the order move to Dispatched status instantly.
  5. 5If dispatching in multiple trips, the Balance qty reduces with each dispatch entry until it reaches 0.
You CANNOT dispatch an order unless it has been approved by Komal Jain AND the Sales person has clicked "Allow Dispatch" on their side. If the Dispatch button is not visible, check with Komal.
